Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Can you get me 10,000 followers?

We will not buy them, and nobody should. Purchased followers never engage, which drags down the engagement rate the algorithm measures, so your posts then reach fewer real people than before.

Who owns the account?

You do. We are added as a user and removed when you say so, and you keep every post and photograph. Ask any agency this before handing over logins.

Can you guarantee more customers?

No. Growth and enquiries depend on your offer, your prices, your consistency and platform algorithms that change without warning. We will tell you what is realistic and then report what actually happened.

Do we have to label posts as adverts?

Where a post is advertising or a paid partnership, yes — the ASA requires it to be obviously identifiable. We handle that properly, because the complaint lands on the business, not the agency.

Why shoot content rather than use stock?

Because stock is why small accounts look identical. Photographs of your actual place, products and people are the one thing a competitor cannot copy, and local customers recognise it immediately.

What if a month goes badly?

We tell you, with the reason. A report that is always positive is not a report, and sometimes the honest answer is that a platform change hurt everyone in your sector.
